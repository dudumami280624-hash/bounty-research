## Three non-negotiable standards — 求证 / 求真 / 求实
1. **Verify (求证)**: trace material facts, historic examples, statistics, dates, definitions, and quotations to primary or authoritative sources. State the exact source and what was or was not verified.
2. **Truth (求真)**: distinguish facts, model assumptions, analysis, personal opinion, historical hindsight, uncertainty, and contested claims. Never disguise conjecture as established fact or correlation as causation.
3. **Practical reality (求实)**: accurately explain genuine market mechanisms, caveats, market-specific regulations, and what a reader can test or practice. No slogans without teaching substance; no guaranteed returns or sensational false certainty.
