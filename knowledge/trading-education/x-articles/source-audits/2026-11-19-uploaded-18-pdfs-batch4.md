## Concrete in-file checks and exclusions
- `量价关系的基本形态.pdf`, page 4, describes volume as something that 'never lies' and equates higher volume with new money entering. Treat interpretation as unverified; transactions always match buyer and seller, but volume alone does not identify investor identity, net cash flow, trader motives or future direction.
- `碰撞均线启动点.pdf`, page 3, describes MA10/MA20 retracement zone as a 'sponge' that usually leads to the next up-leg. Use as a pattern metaphor for critique only; no promise or automatic trading rule.
- `均线的经典形态.pdf`, page 3, interprets bullish moving-average alignment as 'big players' taking control; exclude intent inference, preserve the descriptive ordering of moving averages.
- `认识均线，了解均线.pdf`, page 2, loosely describes MA as average 'transaction price'; verify the exact source series and formula before presenting an example, including whether it uses closes, VWAP, or another methodology.
- `趋势交易.pdf` (69pp) and `趋势交易1.pdf` (9pp) share the same initial passages; not byte-identical. The larger file likely a compilation but needs comparison to the numbered modules before any cleanup.
- `识别市场骗线...` and `散户亏损的原因.pdf` are image-only; limited inspected-page clues are not an audit of their full content. Price action can document a false breakout, but cannot alone prove deception or manipulation.
