## English copy

### Both candles reached $112. Which one finished above $110?

Look at the two candles in the chart. Start with the top of each wick. Both reached $112, so both traded above the $110 line. If all you checked was the high, these two sessions would look the same.

Now find the close on each candle. **A closed at $111**, one dollar above the line. **B closed at $107**, three dollars below it. A ended the session outside the prior range. B traded outside it and finished back inside.

Try a follow-up. Suppose A closes at $109 the next day. What can you add to your notes? **It closed above the line on the first day and back below it on the second.** A close gives you a fact about that session. The next close gives you new evidence about whether the move lasted.

When a headline says a stock broke out, find the level on the chart. Check the high to see whether it crossed. Check the close to see where the session ended.

## 中文讲解稿

### 两根 K 线都碰到 112 美元，哪一根收在 110 美元上方？

先看对照图里两根 K 线的最上端。两根的最高价都是 112 美元，都越过了 110 美元那条线。只看到这里，两天给你的答案一样。

再找它们的收盘价。**A 收在 111 美元，高出这条线 1 美元。B 收在 107 美元，低了 3 美元。** A 收在原来区间的外面。B 也曾经越过去，但收盘时已经回到区间里。

再加一天试试。假如 A 第二天收在 109 美元，你会怎么记？**第一天收在线上，第二天收回线下。** 第一天收在上方仍然是事实，第二天的价格告诉你，这次越线没有持续到第二天的收盘。

下次看到新闻说某只股票突破了，先在图上找那条线。看最高价，知道盘中有没有越过去。看收盘价，知道这一天结束时价格留在哪里。
