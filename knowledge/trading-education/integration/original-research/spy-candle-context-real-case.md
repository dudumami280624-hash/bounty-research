## Original teaching interpretation
A green candle can still represent a losing session relative to yesterday. SPY opened below its previous session close, recovered substantially from the opening level, but finished slightly below the previous close. The candle body measures that recovery, not the entire holding-period return. The high and low do not reveal which occurred first. Do not infer a reliable trading signal from this example. Calculations use the provider's rounded inputs; percentages are approximate.
