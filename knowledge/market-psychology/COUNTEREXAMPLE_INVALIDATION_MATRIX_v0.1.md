# Counterexample and Invalidation Matrix v0.1

| Chapter | Claim to test | Counterexample | Invalidation condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Market as Conversation | narratives can reprice assets | fundamentals improve while narrative remains negative | narrative variables add no explanatory value |
| 2 Balance and Imbalance | ranges precede directional repricing | long ranges resolve into false breaks | no repeatable improvement over random range breaks |
| 3 Trend Is a Sequence | persistent moves contain nested structure | choppy markets create whipsaws | sequence rule fails after costs out of sample |
| 4 Exhaustion and Divergence | momentum can weaken before price reversal | momentum divergence continues without reversal | divergence has no conditional predictive value |
| 5 Three Decision Zones | predefined zones improve decisions | zones are redrawn after the fact | pre-registered zones do not improve expectancy or drawdown |
| 6 Macro Tide and Local Wave | macro regime changes local risk | company-specific events dominate | macro variables add no incremental risk explanation |
| 7 Trader as Distorting Lens | biases alter decisions | disciplined rules override stated bias | bias measure does not predict behavior |
| 8 Leverage Changes Geometry | leverage amplifies forced moves | deleveraging occurs without cascade | leverage has no relation to liquidity/return tails |
| 9 Decision Before Prediction | invalidation improves survival | early exits reduce valid trend capture | risk rule worsens results after costs |
| 10 Practice of Not Knowing | uncertainty should reduce confidence | uncertainty is resolved by new evidence | calibrated probabilities do not improve decisions |
