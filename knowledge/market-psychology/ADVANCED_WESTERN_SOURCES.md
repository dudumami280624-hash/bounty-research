# 高级美国投研资料索引

## 市场微观结构

- Larry Harris — Trading and Exchanges
- Joel Hasbrouck — Empirical Market Microstructure

## 组合与资产定价

- Harry Markowitz — Portfolio Selection
- William F. Sharpe — portfolio theory and asset pricing papers
- John C. Hull — Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives

## 量化、统计与因果

- Ernest P. Chan — Quantitative Trading
- Marcos López de Prado — Advances in Financial Machine Learning；使用时必须审查争议和样本外证据
- Robert Shumway and David Stoffer — Time Series Analysis and Its Applications

## 金融史与周期

- Charles P. Kindleberger — Manias, Panics, and Crashes
- Robert J. Shiller — Narrative Economics and Irrational Exuberance
- Carmen Reinhart and Kenneth Rogoff — This Time Is Different

## 公司财务与财报

- Aswath Damodaran — Investment Valuation and corporate finance materials
- Stephen Penman — Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation
- SEC EDGAR 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and investor education materials
- Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters

## 使用规则

这些资料用于补足市场执行、组合、统计、金融史和财报分析。它们不能自动证明交易策略有效；所有数字和结论必须通过 source-ingestion-and-citation、statistics-and-causality 和 grill-with-docs。
