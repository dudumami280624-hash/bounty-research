# The Market Mind — Psychology Bible Core Skeleton v1.0

## Position

An original American-facing investment-psychology book. Stocks, trading and market history are the scenes. The subject is the investor's psychological pain and reaction.

Chanlun and the KK trilogy are source material for human behavior, narrative, wealth anxiety, security, crowd behavior and self-protection. They are not converted into a trading system.

## Ten psychological chapters

1. **The Pressure to Join** — Why rising stocks create urgency and FOMO.
2. **The Pain of Being Wrong** — Why a position becomes tied to identity.
3. **Holding Losses, Selling Winners** — Loss aversion and the need to end discomfort.
4. **The Fear of Missing Out** — Regret anticipation, comparison and scarcity pressure.
5. **When the Story Becomes Evidence** — Authority, confirmation and motivated reasoning.
6. **When Money Becomes Identity** — Status, shame, security and social comparison.
7. **The Urge to Win It Back** — Revenge, escalation and sunk-cost persistence.
8. **Safety in the Crowd** — Conformity, consensus and group polarization.
9. **Hope Under Pressure** — Denial, rationalization and refusal to update beliefs.
10. **Living With the Unknown** — Black swans, uncertainty and emotional overreaction.

## Chapter formula

Psychological pain → stock-market scene → inner reaction → mechanism → behavior → American case → self-recognition → psychological repair.

## Evidence rule

Use US history, behavioral-finance research, SEC/FRED data and market cases to show how psychological mechanisms appear in stocks and crowds. Data is supporting evidence, not a trading strategy.

## Language rule

Use precise, non-accusatory terms: psychological defense, post-hoc rationalization, selective attention, confirmation bias, identity protection and emotion-driven judgment. Do not diagnose or shame the reader.

## Forbidden drift

Do not turn chapters into stock selection, technical indicators, entry/exit systems, portfolio construction, strategy backtests or return promises.

## Verification

Each chapter requires a psychology evidence card, an American stock-market scene, a historical group case, a self-recognition passage, a repair passage, a counterexample and a Psychology Verified grill record.
