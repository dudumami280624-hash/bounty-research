# Benchmark Definitions v0.1

Every experiment must compare the proposed rule against at least two benchmarks.

1. Buy and hold: fully invested over the identical evaluation window.
2. Random entry: matched holding periods and number of trades.
3. Simple trend: 10/50 or 50/200 moving-average rule, fixed before testing.
4. Value baseline: valuation percentile or earnings yield, only where the data exists.
5. Cash/risk-free: FEDFUNDS proxy for uninvested capital.

Report CAGR, volatility, maximum drawdown, Sharpe-like ratio, turnover and trade count. Benchmarks use identical dates and cost assumptions.
