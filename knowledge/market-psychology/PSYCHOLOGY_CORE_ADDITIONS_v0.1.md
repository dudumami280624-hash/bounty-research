# Psychology Core Additions v0.1

These books are added for psychological interpretation only:

- Robert Cialdini — Influence: social proof, authority, commitment and persuasion.
- Richard Thaler — Misbehaving: behavioral anomalies and limits of rational-agent models.
- Jonathan Haidt — The Righteous Mind: group identity, moral narratives and motivated reasoning.
- Eldar Shafir — Scarcity: tunnel vision, cognitive bandwidth and scarcity-driven decisions.

These sources must not be used to create stock-selection rules, trading signals or an investment-performance system.
