# The Market Mind 英文原生资料补充清单

这是一份研究索引，不是整本书复制。阅读和引用时遵守许可、版权和合理使用规则。

## 价值投资与企业质量

- Benjamin Graham — The Intelligent Investor; Security Analysis
- Philip Fisher — Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits
- Warren Buffett — Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters
- Charlie Munger — Poor Charlie's Almanack; public speeches and mental models
- Howard Marks — The Most Important Thing; Oaktree memos
- Seth Klarman — Margin of Safety
- Peter Lynch — One Up On Wall Street

## 市场结构、趋势与投机

- Richard D. Wyckoff — Studies in Tape Reading; stock market technique writings
- Charles H. Dow — collected Wall Street Journal editorials and Dow Theory sources
- Jesse Livermore — Reminiscences of a Stock Operator; use as historical narrative, not proof
- John Magee and Robert Edwards — Technical Analysis of Stock Trends
- James Dalton — Mind Over Markets / Market Profile concepts

## 行为金融与心理

- Daniel Kahneman — Thinking, Fast and Slow
- Amos Tversky and Daniel Kahneman — prospect theory papers
- Richard Thaler — Misbehaving; behavioral economics research
- Robert Shiller — Irrational Exuberance; Narrative Economics
- Hersh Shefrin — Beyond Greed and Fear

## 周期、信用与风险

- Hyman Minsky — Stabilizing an Unstable Economy
- Ray Dalio — public writings on debt cycles; verify claims against data
- George Soros — The Alchemy of Finance; reflexivity as a hypothesis to test
- Carmen Reinhart and Kenneth Rogoff — This Time Is Different
- John Geanakoplos — leverage cycle research

## 美国历史数据与证据

- Federal Reserve historical materials and monetary policy reports
- FRED: S&P 500, federal funds rate, Treasury yields, CPI, credit spreads and VIX series
- NBER business-cycle chronology and working papers
- SEC EDGAR filings and investor bulletins
- BLS CPI, employment and productivity data
- U.S. Treasury yield and debt data
- CBOE volatility and options statistics

## 使用顺序

1. 先用原始资料建立定义和历史事实。
2. 用 Market Structure Comparator 与缠论逐项对照。
3. 用 Behavioral Finance 解释决策行为。
4. 用 Western Investment History 做美国案例验证。
5. 用 Source Fact Checker 复核每个数字和引用。
6. 用 Visual Theory Builder 画原创图表。
7. 用 Chapter Editor 统一中英双语书稿。
8. 最后用 grill-with-docs 进行拷问。

## 证据边界

这些作者和书籍提供思想来源，不自动证明任何交易策略有效。历史回报、传记叙述和投资者自述必须与可复核数据分开标记。
