# Investment Psychology Writing Boundary v1.0

## Locked definition

Stocks are the cases. Buying and selling are the scenes in which psychology appears. The subject of study is the person's psychological reaction.

The book may be written around stocks, trading and market behavior, but it does not teach people how to trade stocks.

## Allowed

- stock-price scenes
- investor decisions
- fear, greed, loss aversion, FOMO and overconfidence
- holding, selling, averaging down and chasing as psychological behaviors
- US market history as group-psychology evidence
- recognition of emotional pressure
- non-accusatory psychological repair

## Not the book's purpose

- stock recommendations
- entry or exit signals
- technical indicators
- portfolio construction
- position-sizing formulas
- trading systems
- return promises
- performance optimization

## Writing test

Every passage must answer: what psychological pain or mechanism is being explained? If the passage instead tells the reader what to buy, sell or trade, move it out of the main manuscript.
