# The Market Mind 核心术语锁定 v0.1

| Chinese source concept | Locked English term | Definition | Invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 中枢 | Balance Zone | A multi-timeframe area where price is repeatedly accepted by both sides | range is too thin, illiquid or selected only after the outcome |
| 分型 | Local Turning Pattern | A local price configuration used to identify a possible turn | does not survive the chosen timeframe or data rule |
| 笔 | Swing Leg | A defined directional move between qualified local turns | endpoints change under hindsight |
| 线段 | Structural Segment | A higher-order sequence built from qualified swing legs | segmentation rule is inconsistent |
| 背驰 | Exhaustion Divergence | Price progress is not confirmed by a preselected participation or momentum measure | divergence persists or indicator was chosen after the fact |
| 买卖点 | Decision Zone | A location where risk, evidence and invalidation can be specified | no observable invalidation or execution plan |
| 走势终完美 | Structural Completion Hypothesis | A working hypothesis that a defined move eventually transitions into another structure | treated as a guaranteed forecast |
| 区间套 | Nested Timeframe Analysis | Comparing structures across timeframes without treating them as identical | timeframes conflict or create hindsight certainty |

## Language rule

Use “hypothesis,” “candidate,” “evidence,” and “invalidation” in English. Avoid “guaranteed signal,” “perfect prediction,” or “certain reversal.”
