# Six Research Gates for The Market Mind v0.1

This document converts the six identified gaps into mandatory gates for every chapter.

## Gate 1 — Data reproduction

Record dataset name, provider, series ID, vintage/retrieval date, frequency, transformation, missing values and checksum. A reader must be able to recreate every chart and numeric claim.

## Gate 2 — Market microstructure

State instrument, venue, timeframe, order/quote/volume source, liquidity conditions, execution assumptions and slippage. Do not infer investor intent from a candle alone.

## Gate 3 — Statistical validation

Define the rule before looking at outcomes. Include benchmark, sample period, out-of-sample period, transaction costs, parameter choices, missing data treatment and uncertainty. Report negative results.

## Gate 4 — Causal reasoning

Separate observation, mechanism, correlation, causal claim and analogy. State the counterfactual and competing explanations. A historical sequence is not causal proof.

## Gate 5 — Theory conflict

For every chapter compare at least two competing models. Record where each model works, where it fails, what evidence would change the conclusion and which model is used operationally.

## Gate 6 — Operational terminology

Every original term must have: plain-English definition, observable inputs, timeframe, decision rule, invalidation rule, example, counterexample and Chinese-English mapping. Terms cannot depend on hindsight-only chart drawing.

## Required chapter status

A chapter is VERIFIED only when all six gates have evidence links. Otherwise it is DRAFT, NEEDS_REVISION or BLOCKED.

## Required evidence record

Each gate must link to a source card, dataset register, calculation or reproducible note. Preserve source status, adaptation status and unresolved objections.
