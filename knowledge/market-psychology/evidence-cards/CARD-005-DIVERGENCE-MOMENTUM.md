# Evidence Card 005 — 背驰与动量衰竭

## Claim

缠论背驰可与 momentum exhaustion 比较，但背驰本身不保证反转。

## Observable form

Price makes a new extreme while a chosen participation or momentum measure fails to confirm. The measure must be specified before looking at the outcome.

## US comparison candidates

- Nasdaq 1999–2000
- S&P 500 2007–2009
- S&P 500 2020 rebound
- Nasdaq/large-cap growth 2021–2022

## Failure modes

- Indicator choice after the fact
- Divergence persists during strong trends
- Price, volume and fundamentals measure different things
- A divergence can signal deceleration rather than reversal

## Status

ADAPTATION; requires fixed indicator, timeframe, benchmark and out-of-sample test.
