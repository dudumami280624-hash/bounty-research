# Evidence Card 003 — 处置效应与止损

## Source material

- User's market psychology materials and first X thread.
- External evidence: Terrance Odean, “Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses?”
- NBER Working Paper 26668 on selling winners and holding losers.

## Mechanism

Investors may realize gains too quickly while holding losses too long. The behavior is consistent with loss aversion, regret avoidance and the desire to protect a preferred self-story.

## Observable behavior

- Repeatedly moving an exit line
- Calling a deteriorating thesis “temporary volatility”
- Selling a profitable position to feel safe
- Refusing to define what would prove the thesis wrong

## US adaptation

A stop-loss is not only a price. It is preselected evidence that invalidates the thesis. Price, behavior, fundamentals, volatility or time can provide the trigger, depending on the strategy.

## Counterevidence and limits

- Stop orders can suffer gaps, slippage or non-execution.
- Holding a losing position is not automatically irrational when the thesis remains valid.
- Tax, liquidity, portfolio construction and time horizon can affect selling decisions.
