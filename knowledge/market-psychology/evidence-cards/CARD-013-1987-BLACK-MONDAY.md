# CARD-013 — 1987 Black Monday

- Status: REVIEWED, chapter use pending
- Core claim: Liquidity and order-flow feedback can create a crash whose speed exceeds the change in fundamental information.
- Model mapping: Balance and Imbalance; Leverage Changes the Geometry.
- US evidence: SEC records a nearly 23% DJIA fall on October 19, 1987, extraordinary volume, order imbalances, reporting delays and later circuit-breaker reforms.
- Primary source:
  - https://www.sec.gov/news/studies/tradrep.htm
- Counterexample: Program trading is not a complete causal explanation; international markets also fell and multiple mechanisms interacted.
- Invalidation: The claim fails if comparable liquidity/order imbalance episodes do not amplify price movement after controlling for information shocks.
- Adaptation: “Liquidity geometry” is the original US-facing term.
- Missing: event-time order-flow and futures/cash comparison.
