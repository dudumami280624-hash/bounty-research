# Evidence Card 009 — 2022 加息与估值压缩

## Case

The Federal Reserve raised rates rapidly in response to persistent inflation; official monetary policy reports document the tightening path and balance-sheet reduction.

## Research question

How do discount rates, duration, growth narratives and crowded positioning change the meaning of a technical breakdown?

## Counterexample

A rate increase does not mechanically imply every stock falls. Earnings, cash flow, sector exposure and starting valuation matter.

## Status

HISTORY candidate; requires FEDFUNDS, Treasury yields, CPI, earnings and index-sector data.
