# CARD-016 — Trend Following vs Balance-Zone Framework

- Status: NEEDS REVISION
- Core claim: Trend following and balance-zone analysis answer different questions: persistence after movement versus structure before and during repricing.
- Model mapping: Trend Is a Sequence; Balance and Imbalance; A Decision Before a Prediction.
- Comparison: Test whether a trend rule, a balance-zone rule and a combined risk rule differ in entry timing, turnover, drawdown and false signals.
- Counterexample: Trend systems can suffer whipsaws in range markets; balance-zone interpretation can overfit chart structure and invite hindsight.
- Invalidation: The combined model has no value if it does not improve out-of-sample risk-adjusted results after costs and parameter lock.
- Adaptation: “Structure-aware trend filter” is a proposed original term, not a claim that Chanlun or trend following are identical.
- Missing: fixed definitions, survivorship-safe universe, transaction costs, out-of-sample test and failure-case chart.
