# Evidence Card 007 — 杠杆与强平

## Claim

Leverage changes the decision geometry: a trader can be forced to sell before a long-term thesis is resolved.

## US historical comparison

2008 illustrates feedback between falling collateral, margin pressure, forced sales, liquidity loss and broader risk reduction. Use Federal Reserve and NBER evidence rather than retrospective narratives alone.

## Psychological mechanism

Loss aversion delays voluntary selling; leverage later removes the choice. The resulting sale can be mechanical rather than a fresh belief about value.

## Counterexamples

Unlevered investors may survive the same price decline. A leveraged position can also be rational when financing, liquidity and risk limits are explicitly controlled.

## Status

MECHANISM supported in principle; chapter claims require instrument-specific data.
