# Evidence Card 010 — GameStop 与散户叙事

## Case

The 2021 GameStop episode is a useful case for retail coordination, short interest, options flow, liquidity, platform constraints and narrative feedback.

## Research question

When does a social narrative become a market-structure event? Which parts are psychology, which are positioning, and which are execution mechanics?

## Required evidence

SEC reports, exchange data, short-interest methodology, options volume, borrow conditions, trading halts and dated public communications.

## Counterexamples

High social attention does not guarantee a sustained price move. A squeeze narrative can be right about positioning and wrong about timing or exit liquidity.
