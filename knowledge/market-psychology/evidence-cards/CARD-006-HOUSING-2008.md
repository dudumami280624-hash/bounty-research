# Evidence Card 006 — KK 房地产主题与美国 2008

## Source comparison

KK trilogy themes: housing, policy incentives, leverage, distribution, social expectations and affordability.

US case: 2007–2009 housing and financial crisis. Primary evidence should use Federal Reserve historical materials, NBER chronology, SEC filings and FRED credit/housing series.

## Mechanism to test

Credit expansion and rising asset expectations can increase construction, leverage and risk-taking; when cash flow and refinancing conditions weaken, balance-sheet stress can transmit through financial intermediaries.

## Do not overgeneralize

China housing policy, land systems, household balance sheets and banking structure differ from the United States. The comparison is mechanism-level, not a prediction that the same path must repeat.

## Required data

Mortgage credit, house prices, delinquency, bank exposure, policy rates, unemployment, securitization and equity-market response.
