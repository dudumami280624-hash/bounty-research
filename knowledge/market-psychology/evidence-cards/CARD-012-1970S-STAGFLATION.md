# CARD-012 — 1970s Stagflation

- Status: REVIEWED, chapter use pending
- Core claim: A market can remain structurally weak while inflation and unemployment rise together; one variable cannot explain the whole regime.
- Model mapping: Macro Tide and Local Wave; Trader as Distorting Lens.
- US evidence: Federal Reserve History documents stop-go policy, oil shocks, entrenched inflation and the later Volcker disinflation.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/great-inflation
  - https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/anti-inflation-measures
  - https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/oil-shock-of-1973-74
- Counterexample: Supply shocks do not always create persistent inflation; credibility and policy response matter.
- Invalidation: The claim fails if inflation, unemployment and asset prices show no regime interaction across the selected sample.
- Adaptation: Use “regime conflict,” not a deterministic cycle label.
- Missing: FRED CPI, unemployment, fed-funds and equity series aligned by date.
