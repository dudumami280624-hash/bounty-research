# Evidence Card 004 — Balance Zone 与 Wyckoff 对照

## Claim

缠论“中枢”可以作为研究性类比，和 Wyckoff 的供需平衡、Market Profile 的接受区间进行比较；不能直接宣布三者等价。

## Comparison

- Chanlun: multi-timeframe structural overlap and balance.
- Wyckoff: supply/demand, accumulation, distribution and effort versus result.
- Market Profile: price acceptance, value area and auction behavior.

## Testable version

A proposed balance zone should show repeated two-sided trade, reduced directional progress, and a later transition whose behavior differs from random range breaks.

## Counterexamples

- A range may be caused by illiquidity rather than genuine balance.
- A breakout can fail without proving the prior zone was correctly identified.
- Different timeframes can produce incompatible zones.

## Status

ADAPTATION; equivalence UNVERIFIED; requires US index and liquid-stock tests.
