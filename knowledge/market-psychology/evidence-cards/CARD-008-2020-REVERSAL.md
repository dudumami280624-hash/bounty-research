# Evidence Card 008 — 2020 崩盘与反弹

## Case

NBER dates the US recession peak to February 2020 and trough to April 2020. The market experienced an abrupt risk-off shock followed by a rapid policy-supported repricing.

## Research question

How do balance zones, trend breaks, panic selling, liquidity and narrative reversal interact when the usual timeframe assumptions fail?

## Required separation

- What was knowable in February–April 2020
- What became clear only later
- Which signals were delayed or contradictory
- How policy and liquidity changed the interpretation
