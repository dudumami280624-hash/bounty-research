# Grill Audit — Chapter 01 Expanded Sample v0.1

- Chapter: The Market Is a Conversation
- Status: PRELIMINARY_CAUSAL_COMPARISON
- Verdict: NEEDS_REVISION

## Evidence reviewed

- 2016–2025 FOMC release-date sample
- 74 matched non-event pairs; 73 usable price pairs
- SEC MIDAS SPY 2022 Q4 metrics
- CPI, unemployment and federal-funds controls
- SEC 8-K filing-count covariate for six major constituents
- Bootstrap and placebo results

## Strongest supported point

Policy announcement days were associated with short-horizon return differences in the tested sample.

## Hardest objections

1. The 5-day controlled effect is negative but its Bootstrap interval includes zero.
2. The placebo result is also negative, indicating possible period or matching-rule effects.
3. SEC MIDAS coverage is limited to a 2022 Q4 SPY slice and does not provide full event-period quote/order data.

## Missing evidence

- Full-period quote and spread data;
- earnings surprise magnitude and announcement timestamps;
- complete index-constituent controls;
- a pre-registered event taxonomy beyond FOMC decisions.

## Decision

The evidence does not establish that narrative caused the price move through order flow. The chapter may use association and event-window language only.
