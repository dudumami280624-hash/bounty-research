# The Market Mind — Ten Psychological Pain Chapters v1.0

| Chapter | Psychological pain | American recognition scene | Mechanism | Self-recognition question | Repair direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buying because everyone else is winning | Reddit and social-media gains create urgency | social proof, FOMO | Would I still buy if nobody knew? | separate desire from evidence |
| 2 | Refusing to admit a mistake | a losing position becomes part of personal identity | ego defense, commitment | Am I protecting capital or my self-image? | name the error without shame |
| 3 | Holding losers and selling winners | a small gain feels safer than an uncertain recovery | loss aversion, disposition effect | Which feeling is driving this exit? | define emotional attachment |
| 4 | Fear of missing the next move | a rally continues and cash feels like failure | regret anticipation, scarcity | Am I afraid of loss or of being left out? | tolerate non-participation |
| 5 | Believing the story more than reality | a charismatic narrative overrides disappointing facts | motivated reasoning, authority bias | What evidence would change my mind? | write the disconfirming fact |
| 6 | Needing the market to prove worth | portfolio results become a referendum on identity | self-enhancement, social comparison | Who am I trying to impress? | detach self-worth from outcome |
| 7 | Doubling down to recover quickly | after a loss, risk increases to erase humiliation | revenge, sunk cost, hot-hand illusion | Is this a decision or an emotional repayment? | pause before escalation |
| 8 | Feeling safe because everyone agrees | institutions and crowds normalize extreme risk | conformity, group polarization | What danger is consensus hiding? | search for the missing voice |
| 9 | Confusing hope with judgment | “it will come back” replaces an honest assessment | denial, optimism bias | What fact am I refusing to name? | replace hope with observation |
| 10 | Panic when certainty disappears | crisis headlines collapse the ability to think | ambiguity aversion, availability | What do I actually know right now? | act from limits, not certainty |
