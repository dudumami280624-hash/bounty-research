# Causal Identification Register v0.1

| Claim | Treatment | Outcome | Counterfactual | Main confounders | Identification requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative changes move prices | narrative shock | return/volume | same market without shock | fundamentals, policy, liquidity | event window and competing-news controls |
| Rates alter valuation | rate change | valuation/returns | no rate change | growth, inflation, risk premium | timing and macro controls |
| Leverage amplifies liquidation | leverage stress | tail return/spread | same shock without leverage | liquidity, volatility, regulation | interaction model and stress-period comparison |
| Bias affects holding behavior | unrealized loss | sell/hold decision | same position without loss framing | tax, liquidity need, information | account-level or experimental evidence |
| Precommitment improves survival | written invalidation rule | drawdown/recovery | no precommitment | skill, selection, risk size | randomized or matched comparison |

A causal conclusion is blocked when the counterfactual or identification assumption cannot be defended.
