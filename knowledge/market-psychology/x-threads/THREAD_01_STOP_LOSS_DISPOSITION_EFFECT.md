# The Market Mind X Thread 01

## Topic

Stop-loss, loss aversion and the disposition effect.

## Status

PASS — reviewed by grill-with-docs; PUBLISHED on X.

## 中文审核稿

### 1/5
你不是不会止损。你是不愿意把“暂时亏损”变成“确认判断错误”。你觉得这笔交易“还没有错”，于是不断移动止损线。那不是耐心，而是在和现实讨价还价。

### 2/5
止损不只是图表上的一个价格。它应该是交易前就确定好的证据：某个价位、某种行为或某个条件，一旦出现，就证明你的判断错了。价格可以触发退出，但退出的理由必须来自你的交易逻辑。

### 3/5
这就是为什么交易者经常过早卖出盈利单，却长期持有亏损单。盈利会立刻让人感觉真实，亏损却让人觉得还有机会挽回。大脑先保护自己的故事，再保护账户。这种优先顺序会悄悄损害交易结果。

### 4/5
入场前先写下一句话：“如果出现……，就说明我错了。”这个条件必须能够观察、足够具体，而且不能由你的情绪决定。如果条件出现，就退出，不要再和自己展开新的辩论。纪律在持仓出现之前就开始了。

### 5/5
目标不是避免犯错。没有一个认真的交易者能做到这一点。目标是让错误变小、变得清晰，并且能够承受。好的止损不是为了预测市场，而是为了保护你继续从市场学习的能力。

## English X Thread

### 1/5 — 220 characters

You don’t struggle to cut losses. You struggle to turn a temporary loss into a confirmed mistake. The position feels “not wrong yet,” so you keep moving the line. That is not patience. It is negotiation with reality.

### 2/5 — 227 characters

A stop-loss is not only a price on a chart. It is evidence chosen before the trade: the level, behavior, or condition that would prove your thesis wrong. Price can trigger the exit, but the reason must come from the thesis.

### 3/5 — 235 characters

This is why traders often sell winners too early and hold losers too long. A profit feels real immediately. A loss feels reversible. The mind protects the story before it protects the account. That order can quietly damage results.

### 4/5 — 228 characters

Before entering, write one sentence: “I am wrong if…” Make it observable, specific, and unrelated to your feelings. If the condition appears, exit without opening a new argument. Discipline begins before the position exists.

### 5/5 — 225 characters

The goal is not to avoid being wrong. No serious trader can do that. The goal is to make being wrong small, visible, and survivable. A good stop-loss does not predict the market. It protects your ability to learn from it.
