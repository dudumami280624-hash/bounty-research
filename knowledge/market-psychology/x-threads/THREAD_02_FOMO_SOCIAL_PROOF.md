# THREAD 02 — THE PRESSURE TO JOIN

- Status: DRAFT
- Topic: FOMO, social proof and buying pressure
- Psychology chapter: Chapter 01 — The Pressure to Join
- Thread ID: 02

## Chinese review

### 1/5
当所有人都在赚钱时，你突然想买入，未必是因为你看懂了股票。你可能只是开始害怕：如果现在不进去，别人会继续前进，而你会被留在原地。

### 2/5
社交媒体把别人的盈利截图变成了你的心理压力。你看到的是结果，却感受到一种紧迫感：现在不行动，就会永远错过。

### 3/5
这不是简单的贪婪。它可能是社会比较、从众和稀缺感共同作用的结果。市场机会被包装成身份问题：聪明的人已经进场，只有你还在犹豫。

### 4/5
问自己：如果没人知道我买了这只股票，我还会想买吗？如果答案改变了，推动你的可能不是判断，而是被看见、被认可和不想落后的压力。

### 5/5
成熟不是永远抓住机会，而是能够承受没有参与。你不需要每一轮上涨都证明自己。离开一场不理解的上涨，也是一种心理自由。

## English publication

### 1/5
When everyone seems to be winning, your urge to buy may not come from understanding the stock. It may come from fear: if you stay out, others move forward while you remain behind.

### 2/5
Social media turns other people’s winning screenshots into pressure. You see the result, but feel the urgency: act now, or you may miss the only chance you get.

### 3/5
This is not simply greed. Social comparison, conformity, and scarcity can combine. A market opportunity becomes an identity test: smart people are already in; only you are hesitating.

### 4/5
Ask yourself: Would I still want this stock if nobody knew I owned it? If the answer changes, the pressure may come from recognition and fear of falling behind, not from your judgment.

### 5/5
Maturity is not catching every opportunity. It is tolerating non-participation. You do not need every rally to prove your intelligence. Walking away from what you do not understand is freedom.
