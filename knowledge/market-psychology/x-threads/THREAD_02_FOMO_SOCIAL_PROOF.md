# THREAD 02 — THE PRESSURE TO JOIN

- Status: DRAFT
- Topic: FOMO, social proof and buying pressure
- Psychology chapter: Chapter 01 — The Pressure to Join
- Thread ID: 02

## Chinese review

1. 当所有人都在赚钱时，你突然想买入，未必是因为你看懂了股票。你可能只是害怕：如果现在不进去，别人会继续前进，而你会被留在原地。

2. 你打开 X、Reddit 或券商 App，看到的不是完整过程，而是别人展示出来的结果。结果越多，等待就越像一种错误。

3. 于是问题悄悄变了：不是“我理解这家公司吗？”而是“我还能忍多久不进去？”

4. 这不只是贪婪。社会比较、从众和稀缺感，会把一次投资机会变成一次身份测试。

5. “聪明的人已经进场，只有我还在犹豫。”这句话听起来像判断，其实可能只是害怕落后。

6. 当很多人重复同一个故事，故事会获得一种证据感。你不再检查事实，只检查自己是否已经站到正确的一边。

7. 买入以后，如果价格上涨，你会把结果当成能力证明。如果价格下跌，你可能开始寻找新的理由保护原来的决定。

8. 这时最有价值的问题不是“它会不会涨回来”，而是：“如果我今天没有持有它，我还会主动买入吗？”

9. 如果没人知道你买了这只股票，你还会想买吗？如果答案改变了，推动你的可能是认可、比较和不想落后的压力。

10. 成熟不是抓住每一次上涨，而是能够承受没有参与。你不需要每一轮行情证明自己。看不懂时离场，也是一种自由。

## English publication

### 1/10
When everyone seems to be winning, your urge to buy may not come from understanding the stock. It may come from fear: if you stay out, others move forward while you remain behind.

### 2/10
You open X, Reddit, or your brokerage app. You see results, not the full process. The more winning screenshots you see, the more waiting starts to feel like a mistake.

### 3/10
The question quietly changes. It is no longer, “Do I understand this company?” It becomes, “How much longer can I tolerate staying out?”

### 4/10
This is not simply greed. Social comparison, conformity, and scarcity can turn an investment opportunity into an identity test.

### 5/10
“Smart people are already in. I’m the only one hesitating.” It sounds like analysis, but it may be the fear of falling behind wearing an analytical voice.

### 6/10
When many people repeat the same story, the story begins to feel like evidence. You stop checking the facts and start checking whether you are on the right side.

### 7/10
After you buy, a rise can feel like proof of your ability. A decline can trigger new explanations designed to protect the original decision.

### 8/10
The useful question is not, “Will it come back?” Ask: “If I did not own it today, would I choose to buy it now?”

### 9/10
Would you still want the stock if nobody knew you owned it? If the answer changes, recognition, comparison, and fear of falling behind may be driving the decision.

### 10/10
Maturity is not catching every rally. It is tolerating non-participation. You do not need every market move to prove your intelligence. Walking away from what you do not understand is freedom.
