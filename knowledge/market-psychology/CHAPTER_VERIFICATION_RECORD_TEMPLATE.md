## Six gates

1. Data reproduction: source, series, vintage, transformation, checksum, reproduction path.
2. Market microstructure: instrument, venue, timeframe, liquidity, execution and slippage assumptions.
3. Statistical validation: benchmark, sample, out-of-sample period, costs, uncertainty and negative results.
4. Causal reasoning: observation, mechanism, counterfactual, competing explanations.
5. Theory conflict: competing models, scope, failure conditions and decision rule.
6. Operational terminology: definition, inputs, timeframe, rule, invalidation, example and counterexample.
