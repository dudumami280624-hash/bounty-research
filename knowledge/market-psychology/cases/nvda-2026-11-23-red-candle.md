# NVDA on November 23, 2026 — a red candle on a big up day

**Event.** Nvidia finished at $165.05 on November 23, 2026, after trading as high as $171.50. The daily candle was red even though the stock finished 31.48% above its previous regular-session close of $125.53 on November 20.

**Reading trap.** A reader who treats red as a negative daily return has switched reference prices. This is a reading error. The chart does not establish that traders made it.

**Verified facts.** Nasdaq-listed NVDA, regular U.S. session, USD. Open $167.04, high $171.50, low $156.93, close $165.05. The close was $1.99 below the open and $6.45 below the high. Return from the previous close was ($165.05 ÷ $125.53 − 1) × 100 = 31.48%, rounded.

**Open question.** Daily OHLC cannot establish whether the high or low occurred first, how often each price was reached, or what motivated the trades. Use regular-session intraday data to check the sequence.

[FinancialContent](https://www.financialcontent.com/quote/NQ%3ANVDA/historical) reports a $167.03 open. The Yahoo Finance screenshot and the two tables above show $167.04.
