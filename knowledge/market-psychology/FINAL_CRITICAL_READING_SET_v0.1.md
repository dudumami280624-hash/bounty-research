# Final Critical Reading Set v0.1

This is the bounded reading set for completing The Market Mind. It is not an open-ended bibliography.

## 1. Market microstructure

- Maureen O'Hara — Market Microstructure Theory
- Joel Hasbrouck — Empirical Market Microstructure
- Larry Harris — Trading and Exchanges

Purpose: define order flow, liquidity, spreads, execution, price impact and forced trading. Supports CARD-013, CARD-016 and Chapter 8.

## 2. Financial econometrics and backtesting

- John Y. Campbell, Andrew W. Lo and A. Craig MacKinlay — The Econometrics of Financial Markets
- David Aronson — Evidence-Based Technical Analysis
- Marcos López de Prado — Advances in Financial Machine Learning

Purpose: prevent look-ahead bias, data snooping, overfitting and false technical claims. Supports Chapters 3–5 and 9.

## 3. Market efficiency and adaptive behavior

- Eugene F. Fama — selected efficient-market papers
- Andrei Shleifer — Inefficient Markets
- Andrew W. Lo — Adaptive Markets

Purpose: force explicit conflict between efficiency, behavioral error, adaptation and structural signals. Supports Chapters 1, 3, 7 and 10.

## 4. Crisis mechanisms

- Charles P. Kindleberger — Manias, Panics, and Crashes
- Carmen Reinhart and Kenneth Rogoff — This Time Is Different
- Gary Gorton — Misunderstanding Financial Crises

Purpose: explain banking, credit, contagion, leverage and institutional response. Supports CARD-011, CARD-012, CARD-013, CARD-014 and Chapters 6–8.

## Completion rule

A title is only marked INGESTED after the repository contains its bibliographic record, accessible text or verified notes, concept map, conflict notes, US case mapping, counterexample and citation status. A title listed here is not yet claimed to be read.
