# THE MARKET MIND — SCOPE LOCK v1.0

## Highest-level purpose

The Market Mind is an original American-facing market psychology book. Its subject is the investor's psychological pain: fear, greed, loss aversion, denial, imitation, FOMO, overconfidence, revenge, hope, identity and the need to be right.

## Source transformation rule

Chanlun and the KK trilogy are source materials for extracting human psychology, social behavior, narrative, wealth anxiety, class identity, security needs, crowd behavior and self-deception.

They are not to be converted into a trading system, stock-selection system, technical-indicator manual, backtest strategy or investment-advice framework.

## Evidence rule

Market data, US history, SEC/FRED/MIDAS records and statistical tests are used to validate psychological mechanisms and collective behavior. They are not used to turn the book into a strategy or performance system.

## Chapter formula

Each chapter must follow:

psychological pain → inner monologue → psychological mechanism → behavioral consequence → American historical or social case → self-recognition → psychological repair

## Forbidden drift

Do not make the main book about:

- what stock to buy;
- exact buy/sell signals;
- technical indicators;
- portfolio construction;
- strategy backtests;
- return promises;
- stock recommendations.

A buy or sell moment may appear only as a psychological decision scene: what the person felt, believed, feared or denied.

## Locked audience promise

The American reader should recognize:

“This is describing my mind in the market.”

## Status

This scope lock overrides earlier interpretations that treated the project as an investment-system or trading-strategy book. Any chapter that drifts into strategy must be revised back to psychology.
