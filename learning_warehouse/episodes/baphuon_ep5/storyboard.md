- 请求规格：50 秒，5 段 × 10 秒，竖屏 9:16，720×1280，24fps，无旁白，无中文配文，安静版（用户自加音乐）。

- 色：暖灰石头＋暗绿苔藓＋金色光＋蓝灰影，古朴调子统一。

## 全集锁定描述（每段复用）

Baphuon temple-mountain inside Angkor Thom: a stepped sandstone pyramid with layered terraces, warm-grey aged stone with dark-green moss patches, a long elevated eastern causeway carried on rows of round columns with a midway cross-shaped pavilion, two libraries in the courtyard, and a summit terrace. No face towers, no causeway guardian statues, no tree roots wrapping the walls, no five lotus towers. Photorealistic cinematic documentary, vertical 9:16, no people, no text, no narration, only quiet natural ambience.
