# Adult Comedy Short Engine

Use this skill to build an adult comedy short that upgrades instead of repeating. It solves the V5 self review item 4 and item 7 problem: uneven satire and a fruit mechanism that appears only once per episode.

## Build the engine

- Write one sentence for the episode engine first. Name one absurd decision, then upgrade it at least twice, changing the place or the price each time, not the joke.

- Write the satire target as a formula before writing jokes. Name the mechanism, saying or system, name the gap between what it claims and what it does, and name a visible action that exposes the gap. Never make the target a person, an identity or a real brand.

- Give each episode at least one fruit body mechanism that pushes causality forward, not a facial expression alone. Do not stack puns in the same episode to meet this test.

- Give each episode at least three recognisable comedy beats. End with a new irreversible consequence, not a spoken summary.

- Check the second beat for every joke. Look for a reaction, a pause or a silence that carries the joke after the first action. Mark a joke without a second beat as needing revision, not as a visual joke.

## Do not copy

- Never copy another work plot, opening line, dialogue, character design, styling or cover layout. Borrow only the method structure, such as hook, upgrade, muted readability and satire target formula.

- Never write that an audience will laugh, that a video will go viral or that followers will grow. Mark audience validation as NOT TESTED until it is actually done.
