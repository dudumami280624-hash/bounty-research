# Retention and Disclosure Tracker

Use this skill after posting a short video, and before posting the next one. It stops a low new account result from being misread as a ban, and it stops AI disclosure and music licences from being mixed across platforms.

## Record only owned numbers

- Record only numbers the platform itself gives you. Use plays, impressions, average watch, completion, first 2 second swipe rate, likes, comments, shares, saves, new followers, traffic source and single video status. Never fill in a number the platform did not give.

- Treat every judgement as a hypothesis, not a verdict. Zero impressions points first to a technical or violation check. High swipe rate points first to the opening. Low completion points first to density. Plays without follows points first to the follow hook.

- Never use third party test pool sizes or retention thresholds as hard standards. They are reference assumptions only, and they do not prove a video is normal or abnormal.

## Pass the pre posting checklist per platform

- Turn on AI generation disclosure on each platform separately. Choose English as the language and keep Chinese out of the video itself.

- Licence music per platform and keep the proof. Never share one non platform licensed track across TikTok, YouTube and Facebook.

- Keep copy free of real brands and real people. Keep the original work evidence, such as scripts, revisions, reviews and final sources, instead of deleting it.

- If the same problem appears in two episodes in a row, rebuild that episode engine first. Do not keep editing the same line.
