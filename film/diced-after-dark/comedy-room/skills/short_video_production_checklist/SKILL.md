# Short Video Production Checklist

Use this skill before calling a short video shootable. It solves the V5 self review item 10 problem: dialogue over time, lost props after 9 to 16 crop, stacked information on a small screen and untested voice timing.

## Check each shot

- Give each shot one main visual action. Write action, dialogue and graphics in order, never in parallel.

- Make a vertical framing sketch first. Keep the main subject and the key prop in the same frame and keep key text away from edges and platform buttons.

- Watch the shot once with sound and once muted on a normal phone size. Check the first frame, the transition and the last frame in both passes.

- Time every line by reading it aloud with a stopwatch and leave room at the start and the end. If a line runs over time, cut an action or a piece of information first. Never change a line the user already ruled on.

- Label every asset as LOCKED or FLAGGED-UNAPPROVED. Never mark a shot shootable while a flagged asset is unchecked.

- Deliver a not tested list with each item marked NOT TESTED. Never write passed for animation, crop, readability, voice timing, character consistency or sound until each test is actually done.

## Evidence grade and limits

- The framing and read aloud methods come from public text tutorials, not from audience data for this project. They do not prove a crop or a timing rule will raise retention.

- Word count is only a reference. Speaking speed, accent and pauses decide the real time, so each line still needs its own test.
