## High concept: THE MOST EXPENSIVE POTATO IN TOWN
Big Potato wants prestige so badly that he agrees to become the restaurant's luxury signature display. Because he visibly is a potato-headed boss, the customers mistake him for the premium ingredient/meal. He actively encourages the misunderstanding until he realizes it makes him a product rather than a person. Cherry exploits it, Pickle enforces it, Basil resents it, Lemon exposes the false promises.
