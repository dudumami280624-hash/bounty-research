# Complete verified video delivery

## Collect every defect before you fix anything

- List every defect you already know from the user and from your own checks and from a real photo comparison before you change any shot.

- Do not deliver a cut after you fix one defect while another known defect still waits in the list.

- Keep the list open until every shot has been checked, and add each new defect to the same list instead of starting a new version for it.

## Fix the cause instead of the surface

- Change the shot or regenerate it when a colour cast comes from light baked into the source, and do not keep regrading the same shot.

- Check each finished shot against a real photograph of the place for shape and colour and condition, and change the prompt when the photograph contradicts the shot.

- Do not splice a clip from another creator into your cut, and do not present AI generated visuals as real filming.

## Check the whole cut in one pass

- Check every shot and every frame of the finished cut in one pass before you deliver it, and do not split the check across passes.

- Do not report a partial frame count as a full check, and do not call a found defect minor or a background detail.

- Fix every defect the pass finds before you deliver, and do not deliver a cut that still holds a defect you already named.

- Leave the cut silent when the user has not approved music, and do not add a soundtrack on your own.

## Sample each shot across time and confirm its place

- Sample at least a head frame and a middle frame and a tail frame of every shot, and sample at least 5 frames when the source carries burned-in text or subtitles, and do not call one clean frame a passed shot.

- Compare frames across each shot to confirm the subject itself moves, and do not judge motion from a single still frame.

- Replace a frozen or low resolution source when a slow push does not give it real movement, and do not keep a still that a push only pretends to move.

- Verify the action element stays inside the cropped frame for the whole shot, such as a rake head and the salt it pushes, and re-crop and re-check before assembly when it leaves the frame.

- Verify where a stock clip was actually filmed from its source description or location before you use it, and do not accept a clip as Cambodia from visual similarity alone.

## Deliver once and decide yourself

- Decide the finished version yourself as director, and deliver one finished cut.

- Do not send the user a series of versions that each fix one problem, and do not ask the user to choose between candidate versions.

- Do not leave the user a choice question about a fix you can decide, and bring the user only a decision that changes a fact or a visual identity.

- Use this skill for video work outside historic temple episodes, and keep historic temple episodes under the Angkor prompt checks skill.

## Available connectors

- Use `muse.connector_search` to find other connectors and `muse.skill_search` to find related skills.

- If nothing here fits, look on the web for a maintained API or MCP server and set it up with [Custom Connectors and MCPs](/opt/muse/skills/custom-connector-setup/SKILL.md), or build a custom connector.

## Alternatives for better results

- Ask the user to confirm the reference film and the places it must show before you start a montage, and keep that scope fixed while you work.

- Use a real component close up as the opening shot when no whole scene fits the vertical frame, and do not invent a place to fill the frame.
