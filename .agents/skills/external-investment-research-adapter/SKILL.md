# 外部投资研究适配层

## Purpose

Use public investment skills as research workflow references for The Market Mind. Combine their data discipline with the project's market psychology and Western writing rules.

## Workflow

1. Identify the question: market environment, company valuation, technical structure, earnings, options, liquidity, or bubble risk.
2. Collect primary or clearly attributed data before interpretation.
3. Separate measured data, model assumptions, historical analogy, and psychological interpretation.
4. For valuation, triangulate DCF, peer multiples, and SOTP only when the data supports each method. Show sensitivity and bull/base/bear cases.
5. For technical analysis, record timeframe, trend, balance area, breakout/retest, invalidation, and liquidity conditions.
6. For bubble risk, collect volatility, breadth, leverage, issuance, valuation, and narrative evidence. Do not score from impressions.
7. Convert the result into bilingual educational content for The Market Mind. No stock picks, price promises, or autonomous trade execution.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer SEC filings, Federal Reserve, FRED, NBER, BLS, CBOE, FINRA, company filings, and exchange data. Treat yfinance and other aggregators as convenience sources and cross-check important claims.

## Output labels

- DATA: measured value with source and date.
- MODEL: calculation or assumption.
- HISTORY: past US market comparison.
- PSYCHOLOGY: investor behavior interpretation.
- ADAPTATION: English cultural rewrite.
- UNVERIFIED: missing or stale evidence.
