# Non-Accusatory Psychological Language Skill v1.0

## Purpose

Describe investor psychology precisely without blaming, shaming or diagnosing the reader.

## Required language

Prefer mechanism-based terms:

- psychological defense
- post-hoc rationalization
- selective attention
- confirmation bias
- identity protection
- emotion-driven judgment
- loss aversion
- regret anticipation
- social proof
- scarcity pressure
- sunk-cost persistence

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not write:

- you are lying to yourself
- you are stupid
- you are greedy by nature
- you have no discipline
- you are irrational
- you are mentally ill

These phrases assign blame, overstate evidence or imply a clinical diagnosis.

## Rewrite rule

Use:

behavior + context + mechanism + consequence

Example:

Bad: “You are fooling yourself by holding the stock.”

Good: “When a position becomes tied to personal identity, psychological defense can turn new negative information into reasons to wait.”

## Chapter requirement

Every psychological claim must distinguish:

- observed behavior
- possible mechanism
- alternative explanation
- evidence strength
- reader self-recognition question

## Tone

The reader should feel recognized and able to examine the behavior, not accused or diagnosed.

This rule applies to all The Market Mind chapters, evidence cards, bilingual X posts and video scripts.
