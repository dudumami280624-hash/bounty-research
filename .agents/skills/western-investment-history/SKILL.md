# Western Investment History

Use primary sources to compare US market regimes: 1929–1932, 1970s inflation, 1997–2000 dot-com, 2007–2009 financial crisis, 2020 shock and 2022 tightening.

For every case record: macro conditions, market structure, liquidity, investor narrative, behavioral error, policy response, outcome and what the framework could not know in real time.

Preferred sources: Federal Reserve, FRED, NBER, SEC, BLS, Treasury, CBOE and archived exchange data. Do not use hindsight to claim the turning point was obvious.
