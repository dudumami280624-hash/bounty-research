# Plain-English Investment Psychology Writing v1.0

## Locked style

Write in plain, natural English that an ordinary American investor can understand on the first read.

## Prefer

- short sentences
- common words
- concrete stock-market scenes
- one psychological point per post
- direct questions
- natural spoken rhythm
- simple explanations of historical events

## Avoid

- academic jargon without explanation
- long abstract sentences
- translated Chinese phrasing
- unnecessary financial terminology
- moralizing or blaming the reader
- dramatic claims that the evidence cannot support

## Translation rule

Do not translate Chinese sentence structure into English. Rebuild the sentence in natural American English.

## Example

Avoid:
“GameStop demonstrated the intersection of retail identity, institutional antagonism, and speculative participation.”

Use:
“During the 2021 GameStop frenzy, one stock became more than an investment. It became a symbol of retail belonging and resistance to Wall Street.”

## X rule

Every English post must be understandable without reading the Chinese version first.
